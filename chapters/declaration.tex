

\large{\bf{Mandatory group declaration}} \\

{\small \hbadness=10000
The individual student is himself responsible to know which aids are allowed, the guidelines for using them,
and the rules related to the use of sources. The declaration must inform the students about their responsi-
bility, and the consequences cheating would entail. Lack of such a declaration does not release the student
from their responsibility.\\

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ |p{1cm}|p{11.5cm}|p{1cm}|}
 \hline  
 
 1. & We hereby declare that our answer is our own work, and that we have not used other peoples sources or received other help than that which has been declared. & Yes \\
 \hline
 2. & \textbf{We further declare that this answer:}
 \begin{itemize}
    \item Has not been used on another exam by another department/university/college domestic or foreign.
    \item Does not reference any other work without being stated.
    \item Does not reference the individual students own earlier work without being stated.
    \item Has all references stated in the bibliography.
    \item Is not a copy, duplicate or transcript of other peoples work or answer.
 \end{itemize}& Yes \\
 \hline
 3. & We are familiar with that violations of the rules mentioned above is considered cheating and may entail cancellation of the exam, and exclusion from universities and colleges in Norway, cf. Universities and Colleges Act §§4-7 and 4-8 and Examination Regulations §§ 31.
 & Yes \\

 4. & We are familiar with the fact that submitted assignments may be checked for plagiarism.
